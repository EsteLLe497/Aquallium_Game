# Real Aquarium Reference Notes V2

Research date: 2026-07-29

The new route uses three real aquariums as references without copying their
floor plans or branding.

## Kamo Aquarium - quiet accumulation and a single climax

Official reference:
[Kuranetarium](https://kamo-kurage.jp/floormap/floor01/)

Useful observations:

- The current official guide describes about 100 jellyfish species.
- Small displays accumulate before the visitor reaches the main spectacle.
- The Jellyfish Dream Theater is a circular tank with a diameter of 5 m and
  about 10,000 moon jellies.
- Cylindrical "Jellyfish Tube" tanks and several 2 m tanks vary the viewing
  angle and scale without changing the basic interaction.

Project translation:

- Stage 2 begins with narrow, dark displays and ends at one circular focal tank.
- The room is a quiet observation stage. It contains no puzzle.
- Lighting uses black surroundings, pale cyan rims, and low-frequency motion.

## Okinawa Churaumi Aquarium - one hero tank as architecture

Official references:
[Kuroshio Sea](https://churaumi.okinawa/sp/area/the-kuroshio/kuroshio/),
[Kuroshio Exploration](https://churaumi.okinawa/area/the-kuroshio/exploration/)

Useful observations:

- The Kuroshio Sea tank has a capacity of 7,500 cubic meters.
- It is presented from a giant frontal acrylic panel and from additional
  viewing positions such as the Aqua Room.
- A separate course allows visitors to observe the tank from above.
- Dedicated seating makes the tank a destination rather than a corridor prop.

Project translation:

- Stage 5 is a tall, narrow destination hall with a long walking axis.
- The tank is revealed gradually along the entire right wall rather than being
  centered at the end of the room.
- Stage 5 and Stage 7 use separate geometry but share the expensive water,
  caustics, and distant-school rendering assets.
- The story no longer uses the tank as a window into another world.

## Niigata City Aquarium Marinepia Nihonkai - environmental sequence

Official reference:
[Floor Map 1F](https://www.marinepia.or.jp/floor/1f.html)

Useful observations:

- The introduction presents multiple coast types before the main tank.
- The Japan Sea tank contains 800 cubic meters of water and approximately 40
  fish species.
- Its visitor sequence moves from a shallow-water slope through a transparent
  marine tunnel to a large offshore viewing face.
- The official description treats substrate, vegetation, shoreline, shallow
  water, tunnel, and offshore water as one continuous environmental story.

Project translation:

- Stages 3-5 move continuously from coast to reef to open ocean.
- The plan uses one guided promenade rather than a hub with branching rooms.
- Changes in ceiling height, light color, sound, and window scale communicate
  progress without adding new gameplay mechanics.

## Combined design rule

```text
small and quiet
  -> bright shallow water
  -> tall open-ocean spectacle
  -> enclosed underwater arch
  -> sparse deep sea
  -> final dark dome
```

The route changes atmosphere more often than it changes game rules.
